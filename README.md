# Metanoia

**Director:** Fernando Zepeda  
**Genre:** Arthouse Drama  
**Format:** Arri Alexa · Anamorphic · 1.85:1

---

A visual storyboard for *Metanoia* — a short film in which a man, at the moment of his death, is given the chance to watch his entire life played in reverse, moving backwards through every stage of his existence, from his final breath to his first.
